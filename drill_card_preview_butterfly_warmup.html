Butterfly Shot Warmup — Tracking Series

G sits in butterfly in the crease. Shooter just inside the top of the circles at 70–80%. Track the puck all the way into the save — glove, blocker, chest, stick. Run all variations in sequence.

Duration
10 min

Focus areas

Warmup Tracking Save technique

Coaching cues

Track the puck all the way in — see it into the save
Exaggerate the lean to the save side
Drive eyes and nose into the save
Shooter at 70–80% — tracking work, not compete

Coach notes

Shooter stays at 70–80% so the goalie focuses on tracking, not pace. Run V1 through V4 in sequence. Goalies tend to guess shot location — this builds the habit of seeing the puck into the save.
