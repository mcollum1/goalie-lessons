Skill work Variation 1 of 2

Pass & Wrap — Slide Series

F1 passes to F2 on the goal line. F2 catches and wraps tight trying to score far side. G butterfly slides to F2 then power slides across the crease to make the save.

Duration
10 min
Difficulty
Intermediate
Zone
In tight

Focus areas

Crease movement Save technique Jam play Post to post

Coaching cues

Track the pass
Quick slide and reaction
Keep everything tight, rotate effectively
Hand out and over the puck
Follow the rebound

Coach notes

Hands should be attacking — out and over the puck, not passive. On a glove-to-blocker side slide, paddle down is a good variation to work in.
