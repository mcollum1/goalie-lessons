Post Entry & Exit — RVH to Powerslide

Continuous loop: shuffle to post → set RVH → bump to butterfly → pivot → powerslide across → recover → T-push back to center. Primes the crease movement patterns used throughout the session.

Overlap — T-Push and Go Call React

G starts in far-side overlap, generates momentum back into post, T-pushes to center top of crease. On F1's 'go' call, locates F1 and lateral releases into the save.

Time on ice
20 min
Per goalie
10 min
Difficulty
Intermediate
Establishes the session's two foundational habits — using the overlap to load momentum into the post, then a hard T-push to depth before a verbal-cue lateral release. One shot, clean and repeatable. G2 watches and identifies the locate habit while G1 reps.

Coaching cues

Use the overlap to load — don't just walk back to the post
Hard T-push to center top of crease — get to depth, don't drift
Eyes find F1 on the 'go' call — locate before you drop, every rep
5 of 6 Skill work 2 shots

Go Call — Lateral Release and Net Attack

G T-pushes to center top of crease. On 'go' call, locates F1 and lateral releases for shot 1. F1 picks up puck below the goal line and attacks in tight for shot 2.

Time on ice
20 min
Per goalie
10 min
Difficulty
Intermediate
Progression from drill 4 — same verbal cue and lateral release, but a second in-tight shot follows immediately. Tests whether the goalie resets mentally and physically before F1 is already on puck 2.

Coaching cues

Eyes find F1 before dropping — don't react to sound alone
Reset immediately after shot 1 — F1 is already moving to puck 2
Track F1 attacking from below the goal line and stay live through the rebound

Lateral Release — RVH Post Entry and Bump Out

G lateral releases to dot angle, rotate-pushes into RVH on the near post. On F1's 'go' call, bumps off the post leading with eyes and hands to the slot shot.

Time on ice
20 min
Per goalie
10 min
Difficulty
Intermediate
Caps the skill block — the lateral release feeds into a rotate-push RVH and a bump-out challenging the slot. Three distinct habits in one rep and the most complex sequence of the session. Only run if drills 4 and 5 are clean.

Coaching cues

Lead with eyes through the lateral release — head is already on the shooter
Rotate push into RVH — fully set before the 'go' call fires
Bump out with eyes and hands, not pads — pad-first means you're already behind
