Backdoor One-Timer — Wrap-Around Read

F1 passes backdoor to F2 for a one-timer. F2 then goes behind the net, picks up a puck, and attempts a wrap-around. G pivots for shot 1 and reads the wrap on shot 2.

Duration
10 min
Difficulty
Intermediate
Shooters
2
🏒 2 shooters
🥅 Net required

Focus areas

Crease movement Save technique Positioning Backdoor Wrap-around Post play

Rep sequence

F1 passes from face-off dot to F2 backdoor
Shot 1 F2 one-timer — G pivots, eyes and hands to F2
F2 skates behind net, picks up puck
G sets post, head-checks to track F2
Shot 2 F2 wrap-around — G reads side, makes save
Play rebound out

Coaching cues

Pivot early to the backdoor — track the pass, not F2's skates
Eyes and hands get to the one-timer before the shot
Track F2 going behind the net immediately after shot 1
Set post clean — don't drift before F2 commits to a side
Stay patient on the post — read the wrap, don't guess

Coach notes

Goalies who guess early on the wrap get beaten short side every time. The head-check before the bump is the key habit — the goalie has to know where F2 is before committing to a side. Run after the pass-slot RVH drill is clean; the pivot demand on shot 1 is identical.
